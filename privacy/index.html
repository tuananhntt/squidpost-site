Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Squidpost ("we") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Squidpost lets you upload a video once, publish it to the YouTube channels, TikTok accounts and Facebook Pages you connect, and view performance data for those posts.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and profile picture from Google sign-in.
  • Connected account information: when you connect YouTube, TikTok or Facebook, we receive access tokens and basic profile data for that account (such as channel or Page name, ID and avatar).
  • Content you provide: videos, thumbnails, titles, captions, hashtags, schedules and campaigns you create in Squidpost.
  • Post performance data: views, likes, comments, shares and watch time for posts published through Squidpost, read from the connected platforms.
  • Technical data: basic logs (such as IP address, browser type and error reports) used to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide the features you request: publishing your videos to the accounts you choose, showing post status and performance in your dashboard, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your information, and we do not use data from connected platforms for advertising.

3. Data from Google and YouTube

Squidpost uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to data Google processes. Squidpost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke Squidpost's access at any time from your Google security settings.

4. Data from TikTok and Facebook

For TikTok we use Login Kit and the Content Posting API to read your basic profile, upload and publish videos you choose, and read the list and stats of your videos. For Facebook we access the Pages you manage to publish videos you choose and read post insights. You can remove Squidpost's access in your TikTok settings or in Facebook's Business Integrations / Apps and Websites settings at any time.

5. Storage and security

Your data is stored with our infrastructure providers (database, file storage and background job services). Access tokens are encrypted at rest, API keys are stored only as hashes, and each workspace can access only its own data.

6. Sharing

We share your content only with the platforms you direct us to publish to, and with service providers that host and run Squidpost on our behalf under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. When you disconnect a platform, we delete its access tokens. When you delete your account or ask us to, we delete your account data, uploaded files and stored performance data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it. To request deletion, email ntank.dev@gmail.com.

8. Children

Squidpost is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If changes are significant, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: ntank.dev@gmail.com.
